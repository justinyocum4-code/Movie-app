          How it works

          New movies and New TV shows list the latest Internet Archive uploads, with posters. Tap a poster to open its details page: movies show the full description with Play and Save to cloud; TV shows show season posters — tap a season to browse its episodes, then tap Play on any episode to stream it. You can also add RSS feeds on the Premiumize website; working feed downloads appear under New from your feeds.
